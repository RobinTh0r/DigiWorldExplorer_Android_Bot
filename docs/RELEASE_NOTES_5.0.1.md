# DigiWorldExplorer 5.0.1 — Multi-Digimon DWS Update

## Digital World Search now supports more partner Digimon

Digital World Search is no longer limited to Botamon's yellow-eye appearance. A new general
shaded-body detector works alongside the established eye detector and allows other partner sprites,
including forms such as WarGreymon and MetalGarurumon, to be tracked while retaining the existing
grid calibration, route planning, item filtering and motion safeguards.

The DWS grid is also released much faster after leaving the board and no longer remains stuck over
menus or result pages.

## The four major Version 5 features

- **Digi Co-Pilot:** connects Bond Rotation, Meat Field, Home rewards and Digital World Search in
  one timed workflow.
- **Dungeon Co-Pilot:** runs selected dungeons with configured attempts, optional Ad-Skip attempts,
  result/reward handling and navigation back toward Home.
- **Bond Rotation:** cycles up to 15 partners, collects Bond bubbles, restores the starting partner
  and persists the 20-minute timer across app restarts.
- **Meat Field:** harvests ready plots, plants available seeds by priority and uses remaining
  watering cans.

## Additional improvements

- Apokalymon is handled once per game day, including its dedicated result screen, and resets at
  08:00 Europe/Berlin.
- Added a freely available main-app switch for the status panel beside the in-game overlay.
- Colourful partner sprites are no longer misclassified into the collectible exclusion list.
- The compact overlay shows clear Start/Stop states and exposes VS/Tower Loop again.
- Added regression tests for partners without yellow eyes and stale text-heavy post-DWS screens.

## Compatibility

The new Co-Pilot workflows have primarily been tested with BlueStacks. Existing DigiWorld grid and
navigation support remains resolution-independent, but initial runs on phones, tablets and other
emulators should still be supervised.
